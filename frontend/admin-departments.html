ℹ️ Finance Department - Default Powers & Permissions

The Finance department is the default department created with every organization and has special privileges:

  • ✓ Approval Authority: Finance users can approve/reject expenses from all departments
  • ✓ Budget Oversight: Full visibility and control over organization-wide budgets
  • ✓ Flag Authority: Can flag/unflag tickets for audit review
  • ✓ Cross-Department Access: View and manage expenses across all departments

Note: Users in the Finance department are automatically granted these permissions based on their department assignment.
